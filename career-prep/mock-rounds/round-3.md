# Mock round 3: Risk quant (40 minutes)

Sit this cold, with a timer, on paper. Keys are in a separate file;
see [the debrief](README.md#debrief) once the time is up.

## Graded

- **M3.1** A $25m position has daily P&L volatility of 0.8% of notional. One-day 99% VaR, normal P&L?
- **M3.2** A $1m position with 1% daily volatility. One-day 97.5% expected shortfall, normal P&L?
- **M3.3** Equal weights in two uncorrelated assets with volatilities 10% and 20%. Portfolio volatility?
- **M3.4** A correct 99% VaR model over 250 days. What is the probability of six or more exceptions?

## Discussion

- **M3.5** VaR is not subadditive. Give an example and say why it matters.
- **M3.6** Your VaR backtest shows too few exceptions. Is that good news?
