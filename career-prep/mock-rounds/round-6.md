# Mock round 6: Trader, execution and market making (40 minutes)

Sit this cold, with a timer, on paper. Keys are in a separate file;
see [the debrief](README.md#debrief) once the time is up.

## Graded

- **M6.1** Fills: 100 at 10.00, 300 at 10.04 and 600 at 10.02. What is the VWAP of the fills?
- **M6.2** You must buy 50,000 shares at 10% participation. The stock trades 2,000,000 shares evenly over a 390-minute day. How many minutes does it take?
- **M6.3** A market is made on the sum of three fair dice. You see one die and it shows 6. What is your fair value for the sum?
- **M6.4** You buy 1,000 at 100.02, sell 600 at 100.05, and the market closes at 99.98. Total P&L, realised plus unrealised?

## Discussion

- **M6.5** I quote you a market on something you can estimate. How wide should your market be?
- **M6.6** A client order moved the price against you as you worked it. Was it the market or you?
