# Mock round 1: Quant researcher (45 minutes)

Sit this cold, with a timer, on paper. Keys are in a separate file;
see [the debrief](README.md#debrief) once the time is up.

## Graded

- **M1.1** Roll two fair dice. What is the expected value of the larger one?
- **M1.2** Flip a fair coin until two heads appear in a row. What is the expected number of flips?
- **M1.3** X and Y are independent with the same variance. What is the correlation of X and X + Y?
- **M1.4** A daily strategy has an annualised Sharpe ratio of 1.0 (252 days a year). Ignoring autocorrelation, what t-statistic does its mean return have after four years?

## Discussion

- **M1.5** A colleague's backtest uses a universe defined by today's index members. What is wrong, and how big can the error be?
- **M1.6** You have a signal with an IC of 0.02 on 500 stocks rebalanced monthly. Is that worth trading?
