# Mock round 2: Pricing quant and strats (45 minutes)

Sit this cold, with a timer, on paper. Keys are in a separate file;
see [the debrief](README.md#debrief) once the time is up.

## Graded

- **M2.1** Zero rates and dividends, spot 100, strike 100, volatility 20%, one year. Black-Scholes price of the call?
- **M2.2** Same setting. Price of a digital put paying 1 if the stock finishes below 100?
- **M2.3** Discount factors are 0.95 for one year and 0.90 for two. What is the simply compounded forward rate from one to two years?
- **M2.4** W is a standard Brownian motion. What is E[max(W_4, 0)]?

## Discussion

- **M2.5** Why does an at-the-money digital have a large and unstable delta near expiry, and how is it hedged in practice?
- **M2.6** How would you check that a new pricer is right before it goes live?
